  IDEA TITLE
  Proposed Solution: AI-driven burn-in screening system for semiconductor reliability
  • Detects early lot-level anomalies and latent defects before they escape into high-risk deployment
  • Combines contextual anomaly detection with predictive drift forecasting to flag unstable devices at 24h/96h stages
  • Provides explainable QA outputs using SHAP and decision rules for engineering sign-off
  Why it matters: conventional static limits miss defects that appear normal early but drift catastrophically later in burn-in.

============================== SLIDE 3 ==============================
  TECHNICAL APPROACH
  Technologies used: Python, scikit-learn, XGBoost, SHAP, Matplotlib, Seaborn, pandas, NumPy
  Methodology:
  • Feature engineering: Δ0–24h drift, % drift, lot-normalized Z-score, stress interaction signals
  • Module A: Mahalanobis + Isolation Forest + LOF ensemble for contextual anomaly detection
  • Module B: Gradient boosting with MAE + Bayesian uncertainty for 168h degradation prediction
  • Decision layer: cost-sensitive Fβ thresholding and audit-ready QA disposition report
